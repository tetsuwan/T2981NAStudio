T2981NA Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates Reversi on the Facebook Instant Games platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect

When you play Reversi through Facebook Instant Games, we may receive or store the following information:

Provided by Facebook

  • Player ID: A unique identifier assigned by Facebook
  • Language preference: When available from Facebook

Stored via Facebook's player data APIs (on Facebook's servers)

  • Game statistics: wins, losses, draws, total games played
  • Win/loss streak (current and best)
  • Statistics by difficulty level
  • Daily challenge progress and streak
  • Language setting

Stored locally on your device (browser localStorage)

  • AI matchmaking preferences (used for difficulty tuning)
  • Cached copy of game statistics for offline display

Submitted to Facebook leaderboards

  • Your win count is submitted to the Facebook Instant Games leaderboard so that other players can see ranking information.

In multiplayer mode

  • Your Facebook Player ID is exchanged with your opponent's device to enable real-time matches. No other personal information is exchanged.

Our game operates under Facebook's Zero Permissions mode. We do not access your display name, profile photo, friend list, email address, phone number, or any personal information beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide and operate Reversi
  • Save your game progress and statistics
  • Display leaderboard rankings within the game
  • Match you with online opponents in multiplayer mode
  • Improve game balance and user experience

3. Advertising

Reversi displays advertisements through Facebook Audience Network. These ads are served by Meta and are subject to Meta's own advertising policies and data practices. Meta may use your Player ID and platform identifiers to serve ads. We do not share your personal data with third-party advertisers directly. For more information, please refer to Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Game data is stored in two locations:

  • Facebook Instant Games' player data storage APIs (on Meta's servers)
  • Your device's browser localStorage (under your control, can be cleared via your browser settings)

We do not operate separate servers or databases for your personal information. Data on Meta's servers is protected by Meta's security infrastructure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties.

The following data is publicly visible:

  • Win counts submitted to the Facebook Instant Games leaderboard
  • Your Facebook display name (as shown by Facebook) when participating in leaderboards or multiplayer matches

In multiplayer matches, your Player ID is shared with the opposing player's device to facilitate the game session.

6. Children's Privacy

Reversi is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area, depending on the member state). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe a child under 13 (or 16 in the EEA) has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Reset your game data within the game settings ("Reset Stats")
  • Remove the game from your Facebook account at any time, which will revoke our access to your information
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your game data

8. Rights for Users in the European Economic Area (EEA)

If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access to your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

Our lawful basis for processing your data is the performance of a contract (providing the game you are using) and our legitimate interests in operating and improving the game.

Data may be transferred to and stored on servers operated by Meta Platforms in the United States. Such transfers are conducted under Meta's standard contractual clauses and applicable safeguards.

We retain your game data for as long as you use the game. When you delete the game from your Facebook account or request deletion, your data will be removed within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
