T2981NA Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates Reversi on the Facebook Instant Games platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you play our game.

1. Information We Collect

When you play Reversi through Facebook Instant Games, we may receive or store the following information:

Provided by Facebook

  • Player ID: A unique identifier assigned by Facebook for this game
  • Display name and profile photo: Shown on the game screen next to your discs, and your name is included in the turn notifications Facebook sends to your friend during a friend match. We do not store them on our servers or in Firebase.
  • Platform and language: Whether you play on iOS, Android or the web, and your language when available

Stored via Facebook's player data APIs (on Facebook's servers)

  • Game statistics: wins, losses, draws, total games played
  • Win/loss streak (current and best)
  • Statistics by difficulty level
  • Daily challenge progress and streak
  • The AI's memory of openings from your recent games (used to vary its play)
  • Number of sessions and games, the time of the last full-screen ad, and whether one is due (used to space out ads)
  • The ID of a point use that is still waiting for our server's answer (so a point is not lost if the game closes)
  • A counter used when you reset your stats, the state of unfinished friend matches, and a marker that a friend match has ended
  • Language setting

Stored locally on your device (browser localStorage)

  • A copy of the items above, so the game works if Facebook's storage cannot be reached
  • Sound setting

Usage events (Meta app events)

  • The game reports in-game events to Meta's analytics for Facebook games, for example: game mode and difficulty when a game starts, use of Undo and Best move, the number of moves and discs at that moment, whether an ad was shown or watched, share actions, purchase steps (shop opened, purchase completed or failed), your platform and the game version. We use them only in aggregate to improve the game, and they are not sent to any server of ours.

Submitted to Facebook leaderboards

  • Your win count is submitted to the Facebook Instant Games leaderboard so that other players can see ranking information.

In multiplayer mode

  • Your Facebook Player ID is exchanged with your opponent's device to enable real-time matches. No other personal information is exchanged.
  • The current state of a match (board position, whose turn it is, the two players' Player IDs and how the match ended) is relayed through Google Firebase Realtime Database so that moves reach your opponent. It contains no names or photos. It is normally removed when the match ends, but may remain if the game is closed unexpectedly.

If you buy points or earn them by watching ads

  • Your game-scoped Player ID, provided by Facebook in a signed form, and a Support PIN we create for you (shown in the shop, used to confirm data deletion requests)
  • Purchase details from Meta's signed receipt: the product, a purchase token, and the time of purchase
  • Your point balance, each point you use (Undo or Best move), whether a purchase was refunded, and when you earned points by watching a rewarded ad (to apply the daily limit)
  • Purchase and refund notifications that Meta sends to our server, which may include the user ID Meta assigns to the purchase

Payments are processed by Meta or the applicable platform payment provider. We never receive your card number or other payment details.

We do not access your friend list, email address, phone number, or any personal information beyond what is listed above.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Provide and operate Reversi
  • Save your game progress and statistics
  • Display leaderboard rankings within the game
  • Let you play matches with your Facebook friends and notify them of your turn
  • Space out advertisements
  • Verify in-app purchases, keep your point balance, and handle refunds
  • Improve game balance and user experience

3. Advertising

Reversi displays advertisements through Facebook Audience Network. These ads are served by Meta and are subject to Meta's own advertising policies and data practices. Meta may use your Player ID and platform identifiers to serve ads. We do not share your personal data with third-party advertisers directly. For more information, please refer to Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Storage and Security

Game data is stored in the following locations:

  • Facebook Instant Games' player data storage APIs (on Meta's servers)
  • Your device's browser localStorage (under your control, can be cleared via your browser settings)
  • Multiplayer match state: Google Firebase Realtime Database, while a match is in progress
  • In-app purchase records and point balances: our purchase server, hosted on Cloudflare (Workers and D1 database)

Our purchase server only accepts requests signed by Meta or by the game, and it stores no names, email addresses or payment details. Data on Meta's servers is protected by Meta's security infrastructure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to third parties. Cloudflare (which hosts our purchase server) and Google Firebase (which relays multiplayer moves) process data on our behalf only to provide those services.

The following data is publicly visible:

  • Win counts submitted to the Facebook Instant Games leaderboard
  • Your Facebook display name (as shown by Facebook) in leaderboards, and to the friend you play with in friend matches

In multiplayer matches, your Player ID is shared with the opposing player's device to facilitate the game session.

6. Children's Privacy

Reversi is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area, depending on the member state). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe a child under 13 (or 16 in the EEA) has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Your Rights

You may:

  • Reset your statistics and progress in the game ("Reset Stats"). This is not a complete deletion; see the Data Deletion page for what is kept.
  • Remove the game from your Facebook account at any time, which stops the game from accessing your Facebook information
  • Ask us to delete your purchase records by sending the Support ID and PIN shown in the shop (see the Data Deletion page)
  • Contact us to request access to or correction of your data

8. Rights for Users in the European Economic Area (EEA)

If you are located in the EEA, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have the following additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access to your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority

Our lawful basis for processing your data is the performance of a contract (providing the game, keeping your progress, and delivering and keeping the points you buy) and our legitimate interests in keeping the game working, spacing out ads, preventing refund abuse, and improving the game using aggregate usage events.

Data may be transferred to and stored on servers operated by Meta Platforms, Cloudflare and Google, including in the United States. Such transfers are conducted under the providers' standard contractual clauses and applicable safeguards.

Retention:

  • Game data in Facebook's player data storage and on your device: for as long as you use the game, or until you reset or remove it
  • Friend match state in Firebase: until the match ends, or longer if the game was closed unexpectedly
  • Purchase records and point balances: for as long as you have points or may request a refund, and as needed to meet accounting obligations, unless you ask us to delete them
  • Purchase notifications from Meta that never become linked to a player: 30 days

When you request deletion, your data will be removed within 30 days, except records we are required by law to keep.
