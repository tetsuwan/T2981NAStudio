T2981NA Studio respects your privacy and your right to control your personal data. This page explains what each option below does and does not delete.

Method 1: Reset Stats in the Game

Open the game, go to the main menu, and tap "Reset Stats". This resets your statistics and progress (win/loss/draw records, streaks, difficulty records, Daily Challenge progress and the AI's memory of your games).

It is not a complete deletion: your Player ID, purchased points and purchase records, your score on the Facebook leaderboard, saved friend matches, and technical counters (number of sessions and games, advertising timing) are kept.

Method 2: Remove the Game from Facebook

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings.
  2. Navigate to "Settings & Privacy" > "Settings" > "Apps and Websites".
  3. Find the game you played (for example, Reversi) in the list of apps.
  4. Click "Remove".

This stops the game from accessing your Facebook information. Data kept in Facebook's own player data storage is handled by Meta under its policies. It does not delete records on our purchase server; use Method 3 for those.

Method 3: Ask Us to Delete Your Point Data

If you have bought points or earned them by watching ads, our purchase server keeps records linked to your game-scoped Player ID. To delete them:

  1. Open the game and tap the shop (cart) button next to your stats.
  2. At the bottom of the shop, note your Support ID and PIN.
  3. Email both to us at the address below.

tetsuwan28go@outlook.com

We delete only the records that match both the Support ID and the PIN, so nobody else can delete your data. Deleting them also removes any points you have left. We will confirm completion within 30 days of receipt.

If no Support ID appears in the shop, we hold no point data for you. You can still email us with any other privacy request.

What Data We Store

  • Facebook player data storage (Meta's servers): your statistics and progress, the AI's memory of your games, session and game counters, advertising timing, and settings. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.
  • Google Firebase: the state of a friend match (board, whose turn it is, both Player IDs) while it is in progress. It is normally removed when the match ends, but may remain if the game is closed unexpectedly.
  • Our purchase server (Cloudflare), only if you buy points or earn them by watching ads: your game-scoped Player ID and Support PIN, purchase records (product, purchase token, time, refund status), the times you earned points from ads, your point balance and the points you have used. No names, email addresses or payment details.
