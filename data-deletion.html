Data Deletion Request

T2981NA Studio respects your privacy and your right to control your personal data. If you would like to delete your data associated with our games, you can do so using one of the following methods:

Method 1: Delete Data Within the Game

Open the game on Facebook Messenger, go to the main menu, and tap "Reset Stats" to delete all your game data (win/loss/draw records and saved progress).

Method 2: Remove the Game from Facebook

  1. Go to your Facebook Settings.
  2. Navigate to "Settings & Privacy" > "Settings" > "Apps and Websites".
  3. Find the game you played (for example, Reversi) in the list of apps.
  4. Click "Remove" to revoke the app's access to your data.

This will remove all data associated with the game from your Facebook account.

Method 3: Contact Us Directly by Email

If you would like to request complete deletion of all your data, please email us at:

tetsuwan28go@outlook.com

Our game does not access your name, so please tell us whether you bought points and, if so, the approximate date and time of each purchase and the device you used (iPhone, Android or web browser). This lets us find your purchase records. Deleting them also removes any points you have left. We will process your request and confirm completion within 30 days of receipt.

Alternatively, you may use the contact form on our website.

What Data We Store

Through Facebook's player data APIs we store your game statistics (wins, losses, draws) and progress. Multiplayer match state is relayed through Google Firebase while a match is in progress.

If you buy points, our purchase server (hosted on Cloudflare) stores your game-scoped Player ID, your purchase records (product, purchase token, time, refund status), your point balance and the points you have used. It stores no names, email addresses or payment details. Method 1 and Method 2 do not delete these purchase records; use Method 3.
